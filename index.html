Our philosophy

A closed system, since 1960.
(The company: since 2025. We rounded.)

In 1960, a man sealed a plant in a bottle, watered it once, and walked away forever. We read that and thought: finally, a support model.

Your business will run the same way — sealed, self-sustaining, and completely unobservable from outside. Is it thriving? Is it a swamp? Nobody can tell. That's the beauty of glass. That's also the beauty of our reporting dashboard.

Some agencies have a mission statement. We have a Wikipedia article someone read at 2am, and nobody had the heart to say no.
